# Tests

End-to-end tests that drive the real UI in headless Chromium. The game itself stays
dependency-free — these are optional and are not needed to play or ship it.

## Running

They need [Playwright](https://playwright.dev). If it is installed globally, link it in:

```sh
mkdir -p tests/node_modules
ln -sfn "$(npm root -g)/playwright" tests/node_modules/playwright
ln -sfn "$(npm root -g)/playwright-core" tests/node_modules/playwright-core
```

`ai-shape.mjs`, `positional-play.mjs` and `on-ball.mjs` run on an instrumented copy of the game (written to a temp
folder) that exposes who has the ball, each computer player's decision tag, and each ball he plays; the game itself
is unchanged. `classic-tactics.mjs` does the same to reach the match's shapes, the positions' instructions, the stars and a man's IQ in his spots, and
`classic-passing.mjs` to set up the one-touch scenes and read the turn penalty, `classic-sprites.mjs` to reach the
players and their baked sheets, `classic-pitch.mjs` to move the camera, redraw and set up shots at the net, `classic-touch.mjs` to set
the first-touch scenes, judge touches, step a man by hand, hold the stick and read the drawn ring and ball,
`classic-shots.mjs` to set up shots, crosses and a run at goal, hold the stick and read the drawn goal, `classic-strength.mjs` to pause the
match and step the bodies by hand, set up a man on the ball with one of theirs behind him and hear of every tackle, `classic-curve.mjs`
to strike crosses and passes, switch offside off and read the ball's predicted flight, `classic-offiq.mjs` to put your man on the
computer too, set the receiving scenes and hear of every touch, header and shot (and where a man goes for a pass), `classic-crossing.mjs`
to reach the Crossing Lab and the side's plan (and a second copy in real time, for presses timed by the clock), `classic-exh.mjs` to reach the match's styles and its kick-off from the tactics screen, `classic-qsubs.mjs` to wear a man out and reach the subs, and the career's plan and its first match, `classic-thru.mjs` to set the scene round their line and reach the through ball's gaps, arcs, choice and strike, `classic-shape.mjs` to set up scenes in a match and read
the side's plan and the block's spots straight off them, `classic-skill.mjs` to put your man on the computer too, reach the players and hear
of every move the computer makes, `classic-moves.mjs` to read your drawings (and the SKILL MOVES page's), start moves, reach the stars' odds, a career's world and the clips (its
frames stepped at a steady 60 a second, so a move plays out the same every run), and `classic-ui.mjs` to end a match at once,
read the match clock, send the other side's men up the pitch out of the way and skip a season to its review.

Each exits non-zero on failure and prints a PASS/FAIL line per assertion.

## Note on timing

The simulation clamps `dt` at 33ms, so a match cannot be fast-forwarded by inflating
frame timestamps — it has to be stepped. The harness pumps `requestAnimationFrame` off a
timer instead of the vsync clock, which plays a 6-minute match out in about a minute.
